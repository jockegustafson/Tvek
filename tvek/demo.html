Tvek scores one speaker: prosody is normalised over that speaker's utterances, so give it at least ~10 utterances (a minute or more). The curve is causal: the value after a word uses only the words so far. Labels are estimates: on radio interviews Tvek separates certain from uncertain at AUC 0.85, but often calls a long, statement-like question "certain".
